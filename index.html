I've spent the last decade building 3D perception systems. I did my Ph.D. at UC Berkeley on 3D object detection for self-driving vehicles — where RoarNet took 2nd place on the KITTI 3D detection benchmark — then five years shipping autonomous-vehicle perception: monocular 3D detection and camera-LiDAR panoptic segmentation at Argo AI, and a Transformer-based multi-modal perception foundation model at Latitude AI. Today I'm a senior software engineer on NVIDIA's Vision AI team, connecting real-time 3D detection and tracking with visual-language systems for video search and summarization.

On the side, I build learned robot systems in simulation — from single-humanoid vision-language navigation to multi-robot collaboration and camera-only manipulation. Demos below.
